Tips for better results

Good lighting
Natural daylight gives the best results. Avoid harsh shadows or direct flash.
🔍
Close-up shot
Fill the frame with the leaf. The disease patterns need to be clearly visible.
🍃
Single leaf
Focus on one leaf at a time for the most accurate diagnosis possible.
📐
Flat angle
Photograph the leaf flat and straight on. Avoid extreme angles or curled leaves.
